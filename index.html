Questions people ask

Something else? Message us on WhatsApp.

Are flights included?

Package prices don't include international flights, so you can fly from any city or use your own miles. Tell us where you're flying from and we'll add flights to your quote.

Can I change the itinerary?

Yes. Every package is a starting point. Add or drop nights, upgrade hotels, or swap an activity for a theme park or beach day. Your trip planner prices each change.

Do you arrange the UAE visa?

Yes. Send us passport and photo scans and our team files your UAE tourist visa. Your passport needs to be valid for at least 6 months from your travel date.

How do I pay?

Pay 25% to confirm your booking. The balance is due 30 days before you travel. If you book within 30 days of travel, the full amount is due at booking.

Is the food veg-friendly?

Yes. Dubai has plenty of Indian restaurants, and the desert safari and dhow cruise dinners have vegetarian options. Ask for Jain meals when you book.

Is Dubai good for kids and parents?

Very. Distances are short and the city is easy to get around. For families we suggest the 6-night Dubai with Atlantis package, which adds a full day at Atlantis The Palm's marine park and waterpark.

What if I need to cancel?

Refunds depend on how close to travel you cancel. Read the cancellation policy, and your trip planner will walk you through it before you pay.
